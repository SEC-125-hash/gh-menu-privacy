Privacy Policy for GH-MENU Manager

Effective date: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GH-MENU Manager ("the App") handles information when you use its features. The App helps users organize mobile network USSD codes, quick codes, and related information.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, the App may process information you enter, such as network names, USSD codes, quick codes, notes, and voucher numbers entered or scanned through the App.

If account sign-in, cloud synchronization, analytics, or crash-reporting services are enabled, relevant account information, app data, or diagnostic information may also be processed.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Store and organize network codes and quick codes.
  • Provide the App's features and functionality.
  • Synchronize information if cloud services are enabled.
  • Diagnose technical problems if diagnostic services are enabled.
  • Maintain security and comply with applicable laws.

4. Storage and Third-Party Services

Information may be stored locally on your device. If the App uses Firebase or another cloud service, information associated with enabled features may be transmitted to and stored by that service.

Third-party providers process information according to their applicable privacy policies. The actual information processed depends on the services included in the App.

5. USSD Codes and Mobile Network Services

The App may allow you to access USSD codes using your device. USSD sessions and transactions are handled by your device and mobile network operator or the relevant service provider. Their own terms, charges, and privacy practices may apply.

The App does not guarantee the security or availability of services operated by mobile network providers.

6. Camera and Voucher Scanning

If you use a voucher-scanning feature, the App may access your camera to scan the information you choose to capture. Any scanned voucher number is handled according to the features enabled in the App.

Do not share confidential voucher details or credentials unnecessarily.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the App's enabled features, or disclosed where required by law.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may be removable through available App or device controls. Information stored by cloud services may require deletion through the relevant account or service.

To request deletion of information associated with the App, contact the developer using the email address below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, contact the developer.
